# Debriefing form (P5, phase 5)

> **Status: PROPOSAL. protocol not deployed** · Source: thesis, Chapter 4, §4.2.5.3, phase 5 (debriefing, 30 min)

[Version française](fiche_debriefing_pqc_fr.md) · [P5 protocol](protocole_en.md) · [P1 debriefing form](../P1_delta/fiche_debriefing_en.md)

The form follows the structure of the P1 one, with a feedback text read aloud, an interviewer record and an optional anonymous questionnaire. Only the feedback text changes. It is read in French. This English version is a working translation.

## Feedback text (read to the participants)

> Thank you for taking part in this study. Each file contained an ML-DSA or SLH-DSA post-quantum signature that committed to a precise content, never shown in clear. For each file, we had prepared two possible contents and two valid signatures for each.
>
> The study measures whether a professional's decision varies with the signature received, once the content is known. This quantity, the institutional extraction capacity, is compared with that measured with classical signatures. We also measured the effect of the aid sheet shown after your first verdict.
>
> In the second part, the verification result was produced by a **simulated** quantum verifier. No such verifier is used today. ML-DSA and SLH-DSA signatures are verified with classical computers. The simulated noise sometimes flipped the displayed result, at most once in twenty in the noisiest condition. This part explores a hypothetical scenario. It does not describe a weakness of the current standards.
>
> All files were fictitious. Your answers remain linked to a code and will never be passed on to your institution. You may withdraw your data until ___ / ___ / ______ **[date to be decided]**.

## Anonymous questions specific to P5 (in addition to those of P1)

1. Did the aid sheet change your reading of the files? ☐ never ☐ sometimes ☐ often ☐ always
2. Did you notice unexpected verification results in the second part? ☐ no ☐ yes, about ____ times
3. In your institution, would a post-quantum signature be accepted as evidence today? ☐ no ☐ I do not know ☐ yes, with expert opinion ☐ yes
