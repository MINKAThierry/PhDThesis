# Prerequisite checklist (P4)

> **Status: PROPOSAL. protocol not deployed** · Source: thesis, Chapter 4, §4.2.4, `Require` line of algorithm `alg:estimation_conjointe` (“institutional agreement, IRB ethics, $n \geq 30$ participants”)

[Version française](liste_controle_prerequis_fr.md) · [P4 protocol](protocole_en.md)

One checklist per institution studied. It is filled in and signed by the coordinator **before any data collection**. Any box left unticked in part A forbids starting.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Institution (code) | `INST-__` |
| Type of institution | ☐ court ☐ regulator ☐ court of auditors ☐ audit firm ☐ other: ________ |
| Context(s) $C$ studied | ______________________ |
| Proof types presented | ☐ ECD ☐ HEL ☐ G16 ☐ PSS ☐ EGC ☐ other: ________ |
| Data | ☐ reuse of P1 data ☐ own collection |

## Part A. Prerequisites of the thesis (blocking)

| No. | Prerequisite | Supporting document | Done |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Prior institutional agreement | signed agreement ([template](../00_commun/convention_institution_partenaire_en.md)), reference ______ | ☐ |
| A2 | Favourable opinion of a research ethics committee | opinion no. ______ of ___ / ___ / ______ ([file](../00_commun/dossier_comite_ethique_en.md)) | ☐ |
| A3 | At least 30 participants **planned** | planned number: ____ | ☐ |

The actual number is checked again after collection, on participants with at least one usable answer. If it falls below 30, the report says so at the top and the estimates are presented as exploratory.
