# Recruitment and quota tracking sheet

> **Status: PROPOSAL. protocol not deployed** · Source: thesis, Chapter 4, §4.2.1.2 (table `tab:stratification_delta`) and §4.2.2.4

[Version française](fiche_recrutement_stratification_fr.md)

This sheet is kept by the field coordinator. It contains no names. The link between code and identity is kept separately, in encrypted form, by the scientific lead only.

## 1. Pseudonymised identification codes

| Element | Format | Example |
|---|---|---|
| P1 participant | `P1-<stratum>-<3-digit number>` | `P1-JG-007` |
| P2 expert | `P2-EX-<2-digit number>` | `P2-EX-14` |
| Interviewer | `ENQ-<2-digit number>` | `ENQ-03` |
| Gold-standard expert | `GS-A`, `GS-B`, arbitrator `GS-ARB` | `GS-A` |
| Institution (internal use, never published) | `INST-<2-digit number>` | `INST-05` |

Numbers are assigned in the order in which consent is signed. A number is never reassigned, even after withdrawal.

### Recruitment rules

- Check membership of the stratum with questions A1 to A4 of the [profile questionnaire](questionnaire_profil_participant_en.md) before assigning the code.
- A participant who changes stratum during the study stays in the original stratum.
- Replacement of a participant who withdraws before phase 1 is allowed. After phase 1, no replacement **[to be decided by the team]**.
- Any assignment outside the quota is recorded on a [deviation form](fiche_ecart_protocole_en.md).
