undertake, as part of my involvement in the programme for measuring institutional parameters led by LIMSI (ENSP, University of Yaoundé I), to comply with the following obligations.

## 1. Identity of participants

☐ Disclose to no one, including the participants' home institution, the identity of the persons who accepted or declined to take part, or their code.
☐ Write no name, no signature and no distinctive mark on questionnaires, session logs or data files.
☐ Hand the signed consent forms to the scientific lead only, in a sealed envelope, on the same day.

## 2. Answers and data

☐ Make no comment to the participant on any of their answers during the session.
☐ Copy, photograph or transfer no data outside the media designated by the team.
☐ Keep no data on personal equipment after the session.
☐ Report immediately to the scientific lead any loss, theft or unauthorised access.

## 3. Stimuli and ground truth

☐ Reveal to no participant, before their debriefing, the message $m$ associated with a stimulus, the presence of an anomaly in a given case or the gold-standard verdicts.
☐ Not circulate the stimulus sheets outside the team before data collection closes.

## 4. Undertakings specific to gold-standard experts

☐ Give my verdicts independently, without consulting the other expert before handing in my grid.
☐ Not see, and not seek to see, the participants' answers while setting the gold standard.
☐ Declare any interest linking me to a participating institution: ☐ none ☐ yes, specify: ____________________

## 5. Duration

These obligations apply for the whole duration of the study and ____ years after its closure **[to be decided by the team]**. Regarding the identity of participants, they have no time limit.

## 6. Sanctions

I acknowledge that any breach may lead to my immediate removal from the study and to a report to the ethics committee, without prejudice to legal proceedings.
