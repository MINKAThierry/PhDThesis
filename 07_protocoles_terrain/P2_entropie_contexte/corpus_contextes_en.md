# Corpus of the 100 contexts (P2, MDS approach)

> **Status: PROPOSAL — protocol not deployed** · Source: thesis, Chapter 4, §4.2.2.3 (“a set of $n_{\text{ctx}} = 100$ varied contexts”)

[Version française](corpus_contextes_fr.md) · [Dissimilarity protocol](protocole_dissimilarite_mds_en.md)

File produced by [generer_corpus.py](generer_corpus.py), do not edit by hand. Each context combines one level of six elements of the [decomposition grid](grille_decomposition_structurelle_en.md), two per component L, T and R. The combinations are drawn without repetition with a fixed seed. The levels of each context are in [corpus_contextes.csv](corpus_contextes.csv). They are used afterwards to compare the MDS dimensions with the grid elements. The experts do not see them.

The wording is deliberately uniform, so that the judged dissimilarity bears on content and not on style. The team may rewrite it provided a common form is kept **[to be decided by the team]**. The experts work on the French version. All contexts are fictitious.
