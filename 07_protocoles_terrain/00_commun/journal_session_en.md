# Interviewer session log

> **Status: PROPOSAL — protocol not deployed** · Source: thesis, Chapter 4, §4.2.1.3 (phases 0 to 4) and §4.2.1.4 (recording of times and confidence)

[Version française](journal_session_fr.md)
