# Results specific to the thesis

> **Status: PROPOSAL. thesis result** · Source: thesis, Chapters 2 to 4

[Version française](README_fr.md)

This folder lists what the thesis adds or refines with respect to the three published articles of [`../01_publie/`](../01_publie/). The articles remain as the publisher published them. The statements listed here have not been published in a journal; they appear in the submitted manuscript and keep the status of proposals until the defence. Some of them also belong to the refined statements submitted for P2 and P3, which themselves have the status of proposals.

## How to read the entries

Each entry refers to the LaTeX label of the statement, which can be found by searching [`../00_these/manuscrit/`](../00_these/manuscrit/), and to its number in the submitted PDF. Entries R9 and R14 belong to the illustrative calibration, R13 to the construction and the conjecture, R15 and R16 to the undeployed protocols. No statement in this folder is presented as empirically validated. Deployed systems are not instances of the theorem and no measurement protocol has been deployed.
