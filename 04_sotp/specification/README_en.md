No statement is modified. A final section of transcription notes keeps note T4 on the JL dimension notation, now resolved by the revised manuscript ($d$ embedding dimension, $k$ target dimension).

Opposability lower bounds appear as unproven design heuristics, universal optimality as a conjecture and numerical values as illustrations. The status table is in the [parent folder README](../README_en.md).
