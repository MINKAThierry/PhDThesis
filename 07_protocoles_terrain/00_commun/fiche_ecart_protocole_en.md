# Protocol deviation report form

> **Status: PROPOSAL — protocol not deployed** · Source: thesis, Chapter 4, §4.2.4 (reproducibility of the standardised protocol) and §4.2.7.3 (criteria fixed before any data collection)

[Version française](fiche_ecart_protocole_fr.md)

The thesis bases reproducibility on applying the same steps to the same inputs (§4.2.4) and fixes the refutation criteria before any data collection (§4.2.7.3). Every deviation from the pre-registered protocol must therefore be declared, even a minor one. One form per deviation. Forms are numbered and attached to the final report.
