# Test-retest form (P1, phase 2)

> **Status: PROPOSAL. protocol not deployed** · Source: thesis, Chapter 4, §4.2.1.3, phase 2 (“re-presentation of 3 contexts already seen after a 2-week delay”, 1 h); thresholds ICC $\geq 0.8$ and $\kappa \geq 0.7$ (§4.2.4)

[Version française](fiche_test_retest_fr.md) · [P1 protocol](protocole_en.md) · [Analysis plan, section 7](../00_commun/plan_analyse_statistique_en.md#7-reliability)

One form per participant. The coordinator prepares it at the end of phase 1 and hands it to the phase 2 interviewer. The participant does not see it.

## 1. Identification

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Participant code | `P1-____-___` |
| Phase 1 session | `S-____` of ___ / ___ / ______ |
| Phase 2 session | `S-____` of ___ / ___ / ______ |
| Actual delay (days) | ____ (target 14, tolerance ± ____ days **[to be decided by the team]**) |
| Phase 2 interviewer | `ENQ-__` |

A delay outside the tolerance is declared in a [deviation form](../00_commun/fiche_ecart_protocole_en.md).

## 2. Choice of the three contexts presented again

Proposed rule **[to be decided by the team]**. Among the 10 contexts seen in phase 1, three different proof types are drawn at random, then one context per type, drawn at random. Contexts excluded in phase 1 (at least 2 attention-check failures) are not eligible.

**Each context is presented again with the same proof variant as in phase 1.** Test-retest measures the stability of the answer to an identical stimulus.

| Phase 2 rank | Stimulus | Variant $\sigma$ | Phase 1 rank | Q01 verdict in phase 1 (filled in after the session) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `___-C__` | `___-C__-S_` | ____ | ____ |
| 2 | `___-C__` | `___-C__-S_` | ____ | ____ |
| 3 | `___-C__` | `___-C__-S_` | ____ | ____ |

Draw made by ______ on ___ / ___ / ______, seed or method: ____________

The presentation order in phase 2 differs from that of phase 1. The column “Q01 verdict in phase 1” is filled in only after the phase 2 session, so that the interviewer cannot influence the participant.

## 3. Procedure (1 h)

| Duration | Step |
|---|---|
| 5 min | Welcome, reminder of the right to withdraw, reminder of the questionnaire instructions |
| 3 × 15 min | For each context, hand out the file then the [questionnaire](questionnaire_extraction_verdict_en.md) with `phase = 2`, record the times |
| 10 min | Closing question (section 4), thanks, debriefing appointment |

## 4. Closing question

Asked orally, answer recorded by the interviewer.

“Did you recognise any of the files presented today?”

☐ none ☐ one ☐ two ☐ all three ☐ does not know

“Have you discussed these files with anyone since the first session?”

☐ no ☐ yes, with a participant ☐ yes, with someone else

These answers are reported descriptively. A discussion with another participant is declared in a deviation form.

## 5. Computed indicators

| Indicator | Data | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| ICC(2,1), absolute agreement | mean score of the 22 dimension items, phase 1 versus phase 2 | $\geq 0.8$ |
| ICC(2,1), absolute agreement | confidence Q26, phase 1 versus phase 2 | $\geq 0.8$ |
| Cohen's $\kappa$ | overall verdict Q01, phase 1 versus phase 2 | $\geq 0.7$ |

The indicators are computed over all participants, then by stratum for descriptive purposes. If a threshold is not met, the estimates of $\Delta$ are reported with the mention “insufficient reliability” ([analysis plan](../00_commun/plan_analyse_statistique_en.md), section 7).
