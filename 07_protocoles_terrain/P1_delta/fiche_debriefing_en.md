# Debriefing and feedback form (P1, phase 4)

> **Status: PROPOSAL — protocol not deployed** · Source: thesis, Chapter 4, §4.2.1.3, phase 4 (“debriefing and feedback”, 30 min)

[Version française](fiche_debriefing_fr.md) · [P1 protocol](protocole_en.md) · [Information sheet](../00_commun/notice_information_consentement_en.md)

The debriefing takes place after phase 2, so that the design is not revealed before the test-retest measurement. The interviewer conducts it, in a group or individually **[to be decided by the team]**. Part A is read to the participants. Part B is filled in by the interviewer. Part C is an optional anonymous questionnaire.

## Part A. Feedback text (read to the participants)

> Thank you for taking part in this study. We can now tell you more about its purpose.
>
> Each file you examined contained a cryptographic item that committed to a precise content, for example a version of a lease or an electoral choice. This content never appeared in clear in the file. For each file, we had prepared two possible contents and, for each content, two different but equally valid items. You received one of these four items.
>
> The study seeks to measure whether a professional's decision varies with the item received, once the committed content is known. This quantity, called institutional extraction capacity, appears in a theoretical result of the thesis that bounds what a cryptographic proof can convey to an institution. It is neither a test of your knowledge nor an assessment of your institution.
>
> Some files deliberately contained an anomaly in the context, for example a timestamp after the close of a ballot. These anomalies appeared in the description of the situation and never in the cryptographic item, whose verification always succeeded.
>
> The three files of the second session were files you had already seen. This makes it possible to check the stability of answers over time. Three questions were used to check attention. They do not measure your competence.
>
> All contexts were fictitious. Your answers remain linked to a code and will never be passed on to your institution. You may still withdraw your data until ___ / ___ / ______ **[date to be decided by the team]**, as described in the information sheet.
>
> Please do not discuss the content of the files with colleagues who might take part in the study later.

## Feedback of results

The summary of results is sent to the participants who asked for it and to the partner institutions, once the analysis is complete. It contains only results aggregated by stratum, with no individual data ([agreement](../00_commun/convention_institution_partenaire_en.md)).
