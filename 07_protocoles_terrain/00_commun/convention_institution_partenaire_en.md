# Template research collaboration agreement with a partner institution

> **Status: PROPOSAL. protocol not deployed** · Source: thesis, Chapter 4, §4.2.4 (institutional agreement prerequisite) and §4.2.8.3

[Version française](convention_institution_partenaire_fr.md)

The thesis makes prior institutional agreement a mandatory prerequisite (algorithm `alg:estimation_conjointe`, §4.2.4) and the agreement of the courts and regulators approached a prerequisite for deployment (§4.2.8.3). This template applies to a court, a regulator, an audit firm or ENSP itself (protocol P3). It must be reviewed by the legal departments of both parties **[to be decided by the team]**.

## Article 1. Purpose

This agreement organises the participation of staff of the Partner Institution in the research protocol(s) ticked below.

☐ P1. measurement of the institutional extraction capacity $\Delta$ (thesis, §4.2.1)
☐ P2. measurement of the contextual entropy $H(\mathcal{C})$ (thesis, §4.2.2)
☐ P3. measurement of opposability on a Helios election at ENSP (thesis, §4.2.3)
☐ Other: ____________________

The study concerns judgements on fictitious cases. Its purpose is not to evaluate the Partner Institution or its staff.

## Article 2. Undertakings of the Partner Institution

1. Appoint a contact person in charge of practical organisation. Position of the contact person: ____________________
2. Circulate the invitation to eligible staff without naming any participant or exerting pressure.
3. Allow participation during working hours up to ____ hours per participant **[to be decided by the team]** (P1 requires 2 h + 4 h + 1 h + 30 min per participant).
4. Provide, where possible, a quiet room for the sessions.
5. Not seek to know the identity of participants or their individual answers.

## Article 3. Undertakings of the Research Team

1. Obtain a favourable opinion from a research ethics committee before any data collection and communicate its reference.
2. Obtain free and informed consent from each participant.
3. Pseudonymise data at collection and pass no individual result to the Partner Institution.
4. Have every interviewer and expert sign a confidentiality undertaking.
5. Run the sessions according to the pre-registered protocol and declare any deviation.
6. Present the aggregated results to the Partner Institution within ____ months after the end of the analysis **[to be decided by the team]**.

## Article 4. Confidentiality

The parties undertake to disclose no information on the identity of participants or their individual answers. The study stimuli are fictitious and contain no real information from the Partner Institution. The Partner Institution undertakes not to circulate the stimuli before the end of data collection, in order to preserve the validity of the study.

## Article 5. Data and intellectual property

Research data are placed under the responsibility of the Research Team, which keeps them according to the data management plan. After anonymisation, the data may be published under CC BY 4.0. Scientific publications are written by the Research Team.

## Article 6. Naming of the Partner Institution

☐ The Partner Institution agrees to be named in publications and acknowledgements.
☐ The Partner Institution wishes to be referred to only by its category (for example "a civil court").

In all cases, no result is presented at the level of an identifiable institution.

## Article 7. Financial provisions

☐ This agreement involves no financial flow between the parties.
☐ The Research Team covers ____________________ (participants' travel costs, refreshments, allowances).

## Article 8. Duration and termination

The agreement takes effect on signature for a period of ____ months (according to the thesis, P1 is planned over 6 months, P2 over 3 months, P3 over 4 months). Either party may terminate it in writing with ____ days' notice. Confidentiality undertakings survive termination.

## Article 9. Dispute resolution

The parties seek an amicable solution. Failing that, the dispute is brought before ____________________ **[to be decided by the team]**.
